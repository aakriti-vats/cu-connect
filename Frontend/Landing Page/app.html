Quick Help

New to CU-Connect? Here's how to get around. Use the sidebar (or the search bar up top) to jump to any section.

📊 Attendance & Bunk Calculator
See subject-wise and overall attendance. The bunk calculator tells you how many classes you can safely miss while staying above 75%.
🎓 Results & GPA
Track your SGPA, CGPA and grade breakdown per semester. The tips card shows exactly what you need to reach or hold a 9.0+.
📝 Leave Management
Apply for Gate Pass, Medical (ML) or Duty (DL) leave. Every request is routed to its approver and the admin audit store; track its status live.
💻 Online IDE
Write, run and submit code in Python, C, C++, Java, JavaScript or SQL. Submissions are saved under the history (clock) icon.
🗺️ Campus Navigation
Search for any block, lab or hostel and get directions. Use the camera button to locate yourself on the map.
⚙️ Settings
Toggle dark mode, manage notification preferences, edit your profile, and change your password.
Still stuck? Email support@chitkara.edu.in or call the campus helpdesk at +91 1762 507 000.
